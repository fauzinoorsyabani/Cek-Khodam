# Luma Learn — Visual Direction

**Quiet AI learning-operations console.** The product uses an off-white linen workspace, deep ink typography, forest-green primary actions, pale-sage surfaces, and subtle lavender selection states. The visual voice is Indonesian-first, concise, and operational. English is reserved for fixed product concepts such as **AI Review Studio** and **Review Queue**. Every dense operation should feel calm, accountable, and human-in-the-loop rather than decorative or futuristic.
